## Sebastian Raschka

**Summary**: PhD ML researcher and author; staff research engineer at Lightning AI; known for "Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)" (Manning 2025) and bestselling ML books.

- Role: Staff Research Engineer, Lightning AI (focus: LLM implementation and training)
- Prior: Assistant Professor, Statistics, University of Wisconsin-Madison (deep learning research)
- Author of "Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn" (2022)
